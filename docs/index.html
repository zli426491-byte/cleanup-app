隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日

清理大師不會上傳、儲存或分享你的照片和影片。所有分析都在你的 iPhone 上完成。

1. 照片與影片

  • App 需要「照片」存取權限，才能找出重複、相似、模糊的照片、螢幕截圖和大型影片。
  • 分析完全在你的裝置上進行，照片和影片不會離開你的手機。
  • 為了加快下次掃描，App 會在你的裝置上保存分析結果（例如比對用的指紋與檔案大小）。刪除 App 時會一併移除。
  • 刪除照片一律需要你在 iOS 系統對話框中確認，刪除的項目會先進入「最近刪除」。

2. 我們收集的資料

我們不收集你的照片、影片、聯絡人或訊息，也不做廣告追蹤。

訂閱功能由第三方服務 RevenueCat 處理。為了確認你的訂閱狀態，RevenueCat 會收到購買紀錄（例如方案、購買與到期時間）、一組匿名的 App 使用者 ID，以及基本的裝置與 App 資訊。這些資料不包含你的姓名或付款資訊。詳見 RevenueCat 隱私權政策。

3. 訂閱與付款

所有付款都由 Apple App Store 處理，我們不會收到或儲存你的信用卡等付款資料。你可以在 iPhone「設定」>「你的名字」>「訂閱項目」中管理或取消訂閱。

4. 兒童隱私

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童，我們不會故意收集兒童的個人資訊。

5. 你的權利

  • 隨時在 iPhone「設定」中撤銷照片存取權限。
  • 刪除 App 即可移除裝置上的所有分析資料。
  • 如需刪除 RevenueCat 中與你的匿名 ID 相關的訂閱紀錄，請來信聯繫我們。

6. 政策更新

本政策如有更新，會公布於此頁面並更新上方日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Cleanup Master never uploads, stores or shares your photos and videos. All analysis happens on your iPhone.

1. Photos and videos

  • The app needs Photos access to find duplicate, similar and blurry photos, screenshots and large videos.
  • Analysis runs entirely on your device; your photos and videos never leave your phone.
  • To speed up later scans, the app keeps analysis results (such as comparison fingerprints and file sizes) on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.
  • Every deletion requires your confirmation in the iOS system dialog, and deleted items go to Recently Deleted first.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect your photos, videos, contacts or messages, and we do not track you for advertising.

Subscriptions are handled by a third-party service, RevenueCat. To confirm your subscription status, RevenueCat receives purchase records (such as the plan, purchase and expiry dates), an anonymous app user ID, and basic device and app information. This does not include your name or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

3. Subscriptions and payments

All payments are processed by the Apple App Store; we never receive or store your card or other payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

4. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your choices

  • Revoke Photos access at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all analysis data from your device.
  • To delete subscription records linked to your anonymous ID at RevenueCat, email us.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page and change the date above.
